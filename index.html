How to find us

The Cathedral stands on the hill between Newgate Street and Long Street, about a 10-minute walk from the cruise ship pier at Heritage Quay. Visitors enter by the Front Gate.

Taxis from V.C. Bird International Airport take about 15 to 20 minutes, and route buses into town stop a short walk away.
